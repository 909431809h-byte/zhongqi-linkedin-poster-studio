# Zhongqi Poster Brief And Matrix

Use this reference when creating a ten-poster set or when the user supplies a campaign brief. Keep the shared constraints fixed, then choose one row per poster.

## Shared Brief

- 1.91:1 LinkedIn feed landscape.
- Overseas B2B customers in foreign trade, logistics, and freight forwarding.
- Charcoal/deep gray, gray, and white foundation; #FF6602 is a small active accent.
- Keep the upper-left 14% x 18% blank for a real logo and put critical content in a centered mobile-safe zone.
- Render the approved English copy inside the image.

## Creative Matrix

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Global connection | Precise light routes joining quiet nodes | Wide central network | One route endpoint | Confident cross-border reach |
| Ocean trade | Navigation geometry on a calm dark horizon | Low horizon, high negative space | Fine bearing line | Assured ocean-trade coordination |

| Enterprise presence | International architecture, boardroom, or infrastructure scale | Grand but centered, quiet composition | Directional detail | Mature global capability |

## Targeted Repair Prompts

Use one repair at a time and retain all approved parts of the image.

- Text: “Keep the composition unchanged. Render this line exactly once and exactly as written: `<copy>`. Remove all other accidental lettering.”
- Safe area: “Clear every object and all text from the upper-left logo zone. Preserve the rest of the image.”
- Mobile crop: “Move the headline, subheadline, CTA, and focal subject into the centered safe region without changing the concept.”
- Accent: “Reduce orange coverage to a single #FF6602 route/light/CTA accent. Restore neutral charcoal, gray, and white as the dominant palette.”
- Differentiation: “Keep the campaign standard but replace the focal metaphor, spatial composition, and visual medium with `<new lane>`; do not reuse the prior layout.”
